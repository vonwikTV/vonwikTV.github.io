Vonwik TV Setup Guide: Firestick, Login and Fixes

This page is published by Vonwik TV (vonwik.tv). It covers the three things customers ask us most: how to install on a Firestick, how to log in, and what to check when something is not working.

All setup guidesContact support

Install Vonwik on a Firestick

  1. Install the free Downloader app from the Amazon Appstore.
  2. Go to Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options and turn on "Install unknown apps" for Downloader.
  3. Open Downloader and enter the address from our guide: vonwik.app/firestick/
  4. Choose Install, then open the app.
  5. Type the login details from your welcome email exactly as written.

Developer Options missing? Open Settings > My Fire TV > About and click the device name 7 times.

Log in

There are two logins. The app login is the one in your welcome email and is used on the TV. The account login is for billing and uses the email you ordered with.

Login details are case sensitive: one wrong character gives "invalid login". More help: Vonwik login guide.

Not working? Check these 5 things

  1. Status. Any issue on our side is posted on vonwik.live.
  2. Restart. Close the app, then restart the device and the router.
  3. Speed. Run a speed test on the same device. Buffering usually starts below about 15 Mbps.
  4. Login. Re-type the details from your welcome email.
  5. Expiry. Check your subscription date at vonwik.tv/billing/.

Support

Vonwik TV support is live chat, email and WhatsApp. There is no phone line, so any phone number you find online for Vonwik is not ours. We never ask for card details over chat, for remote access to your device, or for payment by gift card.
